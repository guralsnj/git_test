- **Polls open 7:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m.** Anyone in line by 8:00 p.m. may vote.

- **Same-day registration** is available at the polls with proof of residence.
- **This is an OPEN but SINGLE-PARTY primary:** you may vote in only **one** party's column. Crossing over between columns spoils those votes.
- **No referendum questions** appear anywhere on the Marathon County ballot this election.

## GOVERNOR (open seat — Gov. Evers is retiring)

### Democratic primary — contested
**Francesca Hong** — Madison state representative (3rd term), chef/restaurateur, first Asian American in the Legislature, democratic socialist. **Polling frontrunner** (Marquette: 38%; State Navigate: 44%). Raise income taxes on top earners/corporations to fund schools; $20 minimum wage; universal child care; free school lunch; phase out private-school vouchers; expand BadgerCare; rural "hospital districts"; marijuana legalization; **moratorium on AI data centers**; limit local ICE cooperation. Attacked over old posts on police defunding/prison abolition. Backed by DSA chapters, Our Wisconsin Revolution.

**David Crowley** — Milwaukee County Executive, former legislator; the establishment favorite — **endorsed by Gov. Evers**; leads fundraising. Suspended his campaign in July, then re-entered days later. Across-the-board property tax cut paired with a millionaire tax; jobs/vocational training; affordable housing; data centers must pay their own grid costs and use union labor. Backed by firefighter, laborer, and IBEW unions. Polling second.

**Kelda Roys** — Madison state senator on the budget committee, attorney, former NARAL Wisconsin director. Signature issue is reproductive rights; $15 minimum wage bill author; restore public-employee collective bargaining; state **public option** for health insurance; endorsed by **WEAC (state teachers' union)**. Polling low single digits.

**Joel Brennan** — Former state Department of Administration secretary under Evers; ex-Discovery World president. Housing affordability plan; new school-funding formula to cut property taxes; Medicaid expansion + state public option; data-center guardrails. Walked back a remark suggesting Wisconsin wasn't ready to elect a woman or non-white governor. Polling low single digits.

*(Withdrawn but still printed: Mandela Barnes, Sara Rodriguez, Missy Hughes.)*

### Republican primary — effectively decided
**Tom Tiffany** — U.S. Representative for WI-7 since 2020 (gave up the seat to run), former state senator. **Trump-endorsed** and state-GOP-endorsed; presumptive nominee. Freeze property taxes; deregulate energy, housing, healthcare, child care; school choice; crime and illegal-immigration crackdown; voted against certifying the 2020 presidential results.

**Andy Manske** — 27-year-old medical service technician from Franklin; minimal campaign.
